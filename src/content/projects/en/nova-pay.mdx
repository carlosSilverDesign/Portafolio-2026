  problem: "Corporate finance teams executing weekly international payroll and supplier batches faced a 7-step wizard where FX rates expired mid-review. Any single beneficiary IBAN or tax-ID error forced the user to abort the entire batch and re-upload from scratch."

  approachTitle: "My Approach: Two POVs, One Ledger Flow"
  approach: "We architected the payout workflow around the two roles involved in every corporate disbursement: the Treasury Operator preparing the batch and the CFO/Approver signing off on liquidity and FX exposure."
  povs:
    - title: "POV 1: Treasury Operator"
      subtitle: "Inline error resolution without leaving the batch"
      listTitle: "Key design decisions:"
      items:
        - "Split-pane validation grid flagging IBAN/SWIFT/RUC mismatches inline without blocking valid rows"
        - "Partial batch execution: ship valid payouts immediately while queuing flagged rows for review"
        - "Keyboard-first navigation (⌘K actions, arrow-key cell editing) for high-volume controllers"
        - "Automated beneficiary bank routing lookup with instant fee breakdown"
    - title: "POV 2: CFO & Approver"
      subtitle: "Instant visibility into liquidity impact and FX spread"
      listTitle: "Key design decisions:"
      items:
        - "Live 90-second FX quote lock bar showing guaranteed rate vs. mid-market benchmark"
        - "Pre-flight liquidity summary comparing wallet balances before and after settlement"
        - "One-tap hardware passkey / 2FA multi-signature approval drawer"

  challengeTitle: "Core Challenge: Validating the Batch Review & FX Lock Architecture"
  challengeHighlight: "Tested two batch-review architectures with 9 finance controllers: the inline split-inspector reduced error-recovery time by 64% compared to the legacy step-by-step wizard."
  coreChallenge: "When a batch of 150 cross-border payments contains 4 compliance warnings and 3 currency corridors, how do you let the operator fix errors and lock FX quotes without losing sight of the total liquidity impact?"
  options:
    - title: "Option A: Persistent Split-Inspector + Live FX Bar"
      recommended: true
      items:
        - "Sticky top liquidity & FX lock bar visible across the entire review"
        - "Clicking a flagged row opens an inline side-drawer while keeping the table in view"
        - "Instant recalculation of corridor totals as rows are toggled or edited"
    - title: "Option B: Multi-step Wizard + Modal Row Editor"
      items:
        - "Sequential 5-step flow (Upload → Validate → FX Quote → Approve → Receipt)"
        - "Row errors open a centered modal overlay blocking the batch table"
        - "Requires stepping backward to adjust amounts if wallet liquidity is exceeded"
  optionsCaption: "Why these two specifically: Enterprise fintech tools often default to wizards (Option B) for perceived safety, whereas experienced treasury operators need simultaneous context across rows and wallet balances (Option A)."
  findings:
