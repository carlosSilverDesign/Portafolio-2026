During initial exploration, we identified two sides of the marketplace with distinct needs: people browsing emotionally, and shelters managing inventory."

  problemClosing: "To outperform generic classifieds and social groups, the platform needed a clear two-sided architecture: helping adopters find compatible pets quickly while giving shelters a standardized, trustworthy way to publish listings."

  approachTitle: "My Approach: Two POVs, One System"
  approach: "Every architectural decision was evaluated against both user groups simultaneously. Features designed for one side directly reinforced the experience of the other."
  povs:
    - title: "POV 1: Adopter"
      subtitle: "Filtering that respects emotional browsing"
      listTitle: "Key design decisions:"
      items:

    - title: "POV 2: Shelter"
      subtitle: "Structured input without operational friction"
      listTitle: "Key design decisions:"
      items:
        - "Guided listing creation with predefined behavioral and medical attributes"
        - "Consistent data schema that directly powers adopter-side filters"
        - "Centralized dashboard to manage inquiries and listing status"

  challengeTitle: "Core Challenge: Usability Testing the Filter System"
  challengeHighlight: "Validated filter placement via usability testing in Maze, with the persistent sidebar outperforming an overlay modal in preserving user context and reducing task friction."
  coreChallenge: "The filter system was the highest-leverage interaction in the product. Rather than relying on intuition, I built two interactive prototypes in Figma to test how filter placement affected speed, orientation, and user confidence."
  options:
    - title: "Option A: Sidebar filter (persistent)"
      recommended: true
      items:

    - title: "Option B: Modal filter (overlay)"

        - "Triggered on demand, maximizing grid width"

  optionsCaption: "Why these two specifically: They represent opposing architectural trade-offs between persistent spatial context (Option A) and maximum viewport real estate (Option B)."
  findings:
